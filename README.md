# Breaking Bad: An Overview
**Breaking Bad** is a critically acclaimed American neo-Western crime drama television series created and produced by **Vince Gilligan**.
## 1. Basic Information
| Attribute | Details |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| **Creator** | Vince Gilligan |
| **Original Run** | January 20, 2008 – September 29, 2013 |
| **Seasons** | 5 |
| **Episodes** | 62 |
| **Network** | AMC |
| **Genre** | Crime Drama, Thriller, Neo-Western|
## 2. Main Premise
The series centers on **Walter White**, an overqualified but underachieving high school chemistry teacher who, after being diagnosed with Stage III lung cancer, turns to manufacturing and selling crystallized methamphetamine to secure his family's financial future.
## 3. Key Characters
- **Walter White** (Played by Bryan Cranston): The protagonist, who transforms from a mild-mannered teacher into a ruthless drug lord known as "Heisenberg."
- **Jesse Pinkman** (Played by Aaron Paul): Walter's former student and cooking partner.
- **Skyler White** (Played by Anna Gunn): Walter's wife.
- **Hank Schrader** (Played by Dean Norris): Walter's brother-in-law and a DEA agent.
- **Saul Goodman** (Played by Bob Odenkirk): A corrupt but skilled lawyer.
## 4. Critical Acclaim and Legacy
Breaking Bad is widely regarded as one of the **greatest television series of all time.**
> "The show's brilliant writing, complex character development, and cinematography set a new standard for television drama." - *TV Critics Group*

**Awards won include:**
1. 16 Primetime Emmy Awards
2. 2 Golden Globe Awards
3. 2 Peabody Awards
## 5. Spin-offs and Sequel
- ***Better Call Saul***: A prequel series focusing on the evolution of the lawyer Saul Goodman (Jimmy McGill).
- ***El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie***: A sequel film following Jesse Pinkman's life immediately after the events of the series finale.
